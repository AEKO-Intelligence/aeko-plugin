# Saved cached-metric reports

Use this mode only for `ads.performance_report.v1` or `measure.ga4_report.v1` in an `assistant-task-v1` Plan.md. Both save `report_markdown` and carry at most 100 frozen daily metric rows. Read only the listed evidence IDs. Record the exact selected date range, its timezone label, account or property ID, source revision, scanned/saved counts and `coverage.truncated`. These are cached rows, not live provider calls. A missing date or zero frozen rows is unknown coverage, not evidence of zero activity.

For `ads.performance_report.v1`, require `page_id=ad_performance`, `selection={mode: page, resource: ad_insights}`, and the saved `ad_account_id`. The source is unsegmented account-level OpenAI Ads daily metrics. Show impressions, clicks, spend in the saved `currency_code` after converting `spend_micros` by 1,000,000, and calculated rates only with clear denominators. Do not add a campaign/ad-group ranking or claim conversion or ROAS results from these rows; the snapshot has no conversion metrics. Offer at most three evidence-linked next creative or targeting tests for human review, phrased as hypotheses. Account daily rows cannot identify which ad caused a change. Do not recommend budget changes as an authorized action or mutate ads.

For `measure.ga4_report.v1`, require `page_id=measure`, `selection={mode: page, resource: ga4_daily_metrics}`, and the saved `property_id`. The source is cached GA4 AI-referral daily rows by platform and country. Report sessions, orders and revenue with the row's dimensions and date; avoid summing an all-country total together with country breakdowns if both are present. If a currency unit is absent from the evidence, do not invent one. Describe traffic as classified AI referrals, not proof that an AI answer caused an order. Do not trigger a new sync or switch properties.

State the count and date coverage before any period-wide totals. If `coverage.truncated` is true or evidence reads hit the byte cap, present the report as a bounded sample and avoid full-period sums and rankings. Check whether rows are distinct dimensions before aggregating. Cite every metric claim with its frozen `[evidence:<UUID>]` ID; pass only cited IDs to `aeko_save_action_output(kind=report_markdown, schema_version=assistant-output-v1, ...)`. Save and complete under the same claim, reconciling an uncertain output write through task output reads.
