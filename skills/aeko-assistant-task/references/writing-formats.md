# Ad writing-format proposal

This mode applies to `action_id=ads.copy_format.proposal.v1` with `assistant-task-v1`. Verify the owned brand, output language and `selection.resource=ad_copy_formats`. The permitted operation is `save_format_proposal`; the task does not authorize creating or activating a format directly.

Read existing built-in and current-brand formats with `aeko_get_ad_copy_formats` when available to avoid a duplicate name or confusing example. Built-ins `기본형` and `대화형` are writing formats, separate from the older `situation_question` and `conversational` creative strategy keys. Do not relabel an old draft. New users can propose a format with built-in instructions and checks without first creating Wiki or eval documents. Apply any applicable approved brand constraints from an exact pinned package; missing optional customization is not a blocker.

Prepare the structured `AdCopyFormatDefinition` data: `name` (1–100 characters), `title_instructions` and `description_instructions` (1–2000 each), up to five examples with `title` (1–150) and `description` (1–500), and one to twelve distinct BCP 47 `language_codes` accepted by the backend (for example `ko`, `en`, `pt-BR`). Use the brand's supported facts or neutral placeholders; never copy fixture product claims or competitor wording. Save it through `aeko_save_action_output` as `ad_copy_format_proposal` with `schema_version=assistant-output-v1`, `data` only, no Markdown or evidence IDs. The returned AEKO result is for review and apply. Format creation, ad upload, campaign activation and spending remain separate actions.
